How it works

One message.
One booking.

  1. 01

    A patient asks

    "Can I come tomorrow evening?" — at 11 pm, in whatever language they use.

  2. 02

    Real free times

    Clinicoro checks opening hours, holidays, leaves and every doctor's calendar. It never invents a slot.

  3. 03

    Confirmed, not assumed

    It repeats the service, doctor, date and time, and books only after the patient says yes.

  4. 04

    On your calendar

    The booking lands in Appointments and the dashboard, ready for your team in the morning.

Privacy by design

It only ever talks about their own appointments.

  • Tied to the numberA patient sees only bookings made from their own WhatsApp number — never anyone else's, whoever they claim to be.
  • Real times onlyEvery time it offers comes from your live calendar, and it's checked again right before saving.
  • Asks before actingBookings, changes and cancellations happen only after the patient clearly says yes.
  • Your team in controlFlip any chat between Bot and Human, or start the conversation afresh, in one click.
