FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Get in touch.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. Watoro works with the official WhatsApp Business Platform from Meta. We help you connect your business number during setup.

Which languages does the assistant understand?

English, Malayalam and Hindi — including Malayalam and Hindi typed in English letters. It replies in the same language and script the customer used.

What happens when the assistant doesn't know the answer?

It never guesses. The customer is told a team member will reply shortly, and the conversation is handed to your staff with a short note on what's needed.

Can it book a time that's already taken?

No. It only offers times from your live calendar — taking opening hours, holidays, leave and buffers into account — and checks again right before saving.

Can customers see other people's appointments?

No. The assistant only looks up bookings made from the customer's own WhatsApp number and refuses requests about anyone else.

Can my staff take over a conversation?

Any time. Every chat has a Bot / Human switch, and replies from your team appear in the same thread.
