Watoro processes WhatsApp conversations and appointments for businesses. This policy explains what data that involves, why, who receives it, and the rights you have.

1. Who we are

Watoro is operated by Atovian, [registered address — to be added], India ("we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle personal data when businesses use Watoro and when people visit this website.

We play two roles:

  • For business users (the people who sign up and their staff), we decide how their account data is used — we are the data fiduciary.
  • For our business customers' own customers (people who message a business on WhatsApp), the business decides how their data is used. We process it on the business's behalf as a data processor, following its instructions and this policy. If you messaged a business that uses Watoro, please contact that business first about your data.

2. Data we process

Business accounts

  • Name, email address, phone number, role, and login and security information.
  • Business details: name, branches, opening hours, services, staff and settings.
  • Usage information such as sign-ins, actions in the app, device and browser type, and IP address, used for security and support.

Our customers' customers (processed for the business)

  • WhatsApp profile name and phone number.
  • Message content, including text, images and documents sent to or from the business.
  • Appointment details: service, staff member, branch, date, time and status.
  • Any notes, tags or custom fields the business adds.

Website visitors

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, browser and pages requested) for security. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and animation code from Cloudflare's CDN, which receive your IP address when your browser requests these files.

3. How we use data

  • To provide the Service: delivering and storing messages, showing the shared inbox, managing appointments and sending reminders and broadcasts the business sets up.
  • To run the AI assistant: recent messages from a conversation and the business's own information are sent to the AI provider the business has chosen, to generate a reply. The assistant only looks up appointments linked to the WhatsApp number it is talking to.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To provide support and communicate with business users about their account.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use our customers' conversations to train AI models.

4. Who we share data with

  • Meta Platforms — to send and receive WhatsApp messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • AI providers chosen by the business (for example OpenAI, Anthropic or Groq) — to generate assistant replies.
  • Infrastructure providers — database and authentication hosting (Supabase) and our servers, which store data on our behalf.
  • Authorities — when required by law.

Some of these providers may process data outside India. Where they do, we rely on their contractual safeguards and comply with any restrictions notified under Indian law.

5. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), encrypt WhatsApp and AI access tokens at rest, separate each business's data at the database level, and give businesses role-based access controls for their staff. No system is perfectly secure; if a personal data breach affects you, we will notify the business and the authorities as required by law.

6. How long we keep data

We keep account and conversation data for as long as the business's account is active, or as the business decides. When an account is closed, or a business asks us to delete data, we delete it within 30 days, except where we must keep it longer by law. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

7. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise your rights. If you are a customer of a business that uses Watoro, contact that business; we will help them respond. Business users can contact us directly. See Data deletion for how to ask us to delete data.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

Watoro is a service for businesses and is not directed at children. Businesses must obtain any consent the law requires before processing a child's data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new date and, for material changes, notify account owners.

For privacy questions or to raise a grievance: [contact email — to be added]. We aim to respond within 7 days.
